# Product

## Register

product

## Users
JEE aspirants using it as a focused, offline-first study planner and tracker. They need a highly optimized tool during intensive study sessions.

## Product Purpose
To log study time, track syllabus completion across Physics, Chemistry, and Maths, and analyze progress to prepare for the JEE exam. Success means the user feels in control, motivated, and aware of their preparation status without distractions.

## Brand Personality
High-focus, premium, and precise. It should evoke feelings of confidence, clarity, and calm through glassmorphism and clean typography.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS templates, cluttered card layouts, and distracting animations. It should definitely avoid looking like a bloated standard web app.

## Design Principles
1. **Focus First:** Eliminate distractions; every pixel should serve the purpose of studying.
2. **Premium Precision:** Use high-quality visual cues (glassmorphism, tailored contrast) that make the tool feel like a premium command center.
3. **Show, Don't Tell:** Use visual data (heatmaps, progress bars) rather than dense text to convey progress.
4. **Adaptive Context:** Support different environments natively (e.g., light, dark-glass, dark-solid) without losing identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support for reduced motion, high contrast modes (like dark-solid), and standard WCAG AA contrast. Must ensure typography scales and remains readable against dynamic backgrounds.
